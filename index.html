Masculinity and Femininity

People nowadays have more and more freedom in living with their sexual identities, but most of us are trying to follow a standard of genders, which leads to masculinity and femininity. This website is illustrating how these concepts appear in our life and their influence on our beliefs, based on “I Love My Body, but Hers is Better!” by Nicole Taylor, Mimi Nichter (2025) as well as “Boys Will Be Boys” by Jonathon Reed (2014), and Barbie movie directed by Greta Gerwig (2023).
